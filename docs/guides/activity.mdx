**Activity** lists everything Meridian did, and who asked for it. Each of these is one entry: a deploy, rollback or redeploy, a secret set, and a change to a project, token, person or setting. So is each time someone reads logs or traffic.

Everyone who can sign in can read it. See [Roles](/concepts/roles).

## Find an entry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Filters">
    Click **Filters** at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what to see">
    - **Action starts with**: an action, or the start of one. `app.deploy` finds deploys. `app.` finds deploys, rollbacks and redeploys. `secret.` finds every secret set.
    - **Who**: one person, or **Anyone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click **Apply**. The page shows 50 entries. **Older** and **Newer** move between pages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Question | Filter |
| --- | --- |
| Who shipped what this week? | `app.deploy`, `app.rollback` and `app.redeploy` |
| Who rotated a secret? | `secret.set` |
| Who read the logs of a container? | `logs.viewed` |
| What changed in the infra repository through Meridian? | `project.committed` and `project.pull_request` |
| Who changed someone's role? | `user.updated` |

The filters live in the URL, so you can share a filtered view:

| Parameter | Value | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `action_name` | the start of an action | `/events?action_name=app.rollback` |
| `actor` | a person's ID | `/events?actor=3` |
| `page` | a page number | `/events?page=2` |

## Read an entry

Entries are newest first, grouped by day under **Today**, **Yesterday**, or the date. Each entry shows:

- A sentence, such as `Deployed shop · production to v1.4.2`.
- Who did it. A removed person shows as their name with `(removed)`. Work Meridian did on its own shows **Meridian**.
- The action, such as `app.deploy`.
- The commit's short ID, or a **pull request** link, when the action made one.
- The time, as `HH:MM`.

Every action, and what each one records, is in [Activity events](/reference/activity-events).

<Note>
Activity records only what Meridian did. A commit someone pushed to the [infra repository](/reference/glossary#infra-repository) directly isn't an entry. It shows in the app's **Releases**, and in **Current deploy** as by **Git**. See [Deploy](/guides/deploy#follow-a-deploy).
</Note>
