Admins add people to Meridian directly, with an email address, a password and a role. There are no invitations and no sign-up page. People sign in with their email address and password.

**People** is under **Settings**, which only admins can open.

## Roles

Each person has one role: **Viewer**, **Deployer** or **Admin**. A new person is a viewer unless you pick otherwise.

<Roles />

The reasons behind the split are in [Roles](/concepts/roles).

## Add a person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open People">
    Open **Settings**, then the **People** tab, and click **Add person**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    - **Name**: optional. Meridian shows the email address where there is no name.
    - **Email**: what they sign in with. Meridian stores it in lower case, and each address can be used once.
    - **Password**: at least 12 characters, at most 72.
    - **Role**: **Viewer**, **Deployer** or **Admin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create User**. Meridian says `ada@example.com can now sign in.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the password">
    Send them Meridian's URL, their email address, and the password, privately. Meridian sends no email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change a role

Click **Edit** next to the person, pick a new **Role**, and click **Update User**.

The change takes effect at once. Meridian closes the person's open live connections, so their pages reconnect with the new role. A deployer made a viewer stops seeing logs straight away, not at their next sign-in.

<Note>
There must always be one admin. Meridian refuses to make the last admin a viewer or a deployer, with `There must always be one admin.`. Make someone else an admin first.
</Note>

## Change a password

Click **Edit** next to the person, type a new **Password**, and click **Update User**. Leave **Password** blank to keep the current one.

A new password signs the person out everywhere else. Every other session of theirs ends, and their open pages disconnect. When you change your own password, your current session stays signed in.

Only admins can open **Settings**, so a viewer or deployer can't change their own password. An admin changes it for them.

## Remove a person

Click **Edit** next to the person, click **Remove**, and confirm. Meridian ends every session they have, and they can't sign in again.

What they did stays on record, under a different name in each place. Activity keeps their name, marked `(removed)`. A secret they set shows as set by `a removed user`. A deploy they made shows as by **Git** in **Current deploy**.

You can't remove yourself. Ask another admin. With only one admin, that admin can't be removed at all.

## Signing in

People sign in at `/session/new` with their email address and password. A session lasts until they sign out from the menu under their name, or until an admin changes their password or removes them.

Sign-in allows 10 attempts every 3 minutes from one IP address. Past that, the page says `Try again later.`.

The first admin is created on a new install's first run, with the setup code from Meridian's log. See [Install](/install).
