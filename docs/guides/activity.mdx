**Activity** lists everything Meridian did, and who asked for it. Each of these is one entry: a deploy, rollback or redeploy, how CI's run of it ended, a secret set, and a change to a project, token, setting or your account.

## Find an entry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Filters">
    Click **Filters** at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what to see">
    - **Action starts with**: an action, or the start of one. `app.deploy` finds deploys. `app.` finds deploys, rollbacks and redeploys. `secret.` finds every secret set.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click **Apply**. The page shows 50 entries. **Older** and **Newer** move between pages.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Question | Filter |
| --- | --- |
| What shipped this week? | `app.deploy`, `app.rollback` and `app.redeploy` |
| Which deploys failed? | `app.deploy.failure` |
| When was a secret rotated? | `secret.set` |
| What changed in the infra repository through Meridian? | `project.committed` and `project.pull_request` |
| When did the account's email or password change? | `user.updated` |

The filters live in the URL, so you can share a filtered view:

| Parameter | Value | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `action_name` | the start of an action | `/events?action_name=app.rollback` |
| `page` | a page number | `/events?page=2` |

## Read an entry

Entries are newest first, grouped by day under **Today**, **Yesterday**, or the date. Each entry shows:

- A sentence, such as `Deployed shop · production to v1.4.2`.
- Who did it: you, **Git** for a deploy that came from a push or from GitHub, or **Meridian** for work it did on its own.
- The action, such as `app.deploy`.
- The commit's short ID, a **pull request** link, or a **CI run** link, when there is one.
- The time, as `HH:MM`.

Every action, and what each one records, is in [Activity events](/reference/activity-events).

<Note>
A commit someone pushed to the [infra repository](/reference/glossary#infra-repository) directly isn't an entry itself. It shows in the app's **Releases**, and once CI's deploy for it finishes, Activity shows how it ended, by **Git**. See [Deploy](/guides/deploy#follow-a-deploy).
</Note>
