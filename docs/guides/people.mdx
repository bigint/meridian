An admin adds each person to Meridian with an email address, a password and a role. There are no invitations and no sign-up page, and Meridian sends no email.

**People** is a tab of **Settings**, and only admins can open **Settings**.

## Roles

Each person has one [role](/reference/glossary#roles): **Viewer**, **Deployer** or **Admin**. A new person is a viewer unless you pick another role.

<Roles />

The reasons behind the split are in [Roles](/concepts/roles).

## Add a person

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open People">
    Open **Settings**, then the **People** tab, and click **Add person**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    - **Name**: optional. Where there is no name, Meridian shows the email address.
    - **Email**: what they sign in with. Meridian stores it in lower case. Each address can belong to one person only.
    - **Password**: from 12 to 72 characters.
    - **Role**: **Viewer**, **Deployer** or **Admin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create User**. Meridian says `ada@example.com can now sign in.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the password">
    Send them Meridian's URL, their email address and the password, privately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

They sign in at `/session/new`. A session lasts until they sign out from the menu under their name. It also ends when an admin changes their password or removes them.

## Change a role

Click **Edit** next to the person, pick a new **Role**, and click **Update User**.

The change works at once. Meridian closes the person's open live connections, and their pages reconnect with the new role. So a deployer you make a viewer stops seeing logs straight away.

<Note>
Meridian always keeps one admin. It refuses to make the last admin a viewer or a deployer, and says `There must always be one admin.` Make someone else an admin first.
</Note>

## Change a password

Click **Edit** next to the person, type a new **Password**, and click **Update User**. To keep the current password, leave **Password** blank.

A new password signs the person out everywhere else, and their open pages disconnect. When you change your own password, your current session stays signed in.

Viewers and deployers can't open **Settings**, so they can't change their own password. An admin changes it for them.

## Remove a person

Click **Edit** next to the person, click **Remove**, and confirm. Meridian ends every session they have, and they can't sign in again.

What they did stays on record:

- Activity keeps their name, marked `(removed)`.
- A secret they set shows as set by `a removed user`.
- A deploy they made keeps their name in **Current deploy**, marked `(removed)`.

You can't remove yourself, so ask another admin. The last admin can't be removed at all.

## If sign-in fails

- **Try again later.** Sign-in allows 10 tries in 3 minutes from one IP address. Wait 3 minutes.
- **A new install has no one to sign in as.** Create the first admin at `/first_run`, with the setup code from Meridian's log. See [Install](/install).
